13. CRITICAL RCC PILLAR VOLUME & HEIGHT UNIT CALCULATION FIX
================================================================================
- Root Cause Identified:
  * In `calculatePillarsRcc`, pillar width and depth were stored with `p.unit = 'in'` (e.g. 9 inches).
  * `pH_M` (pillar height) evaluated `toMeters(p.height || floorHeight, p.unit || buildingUnit)`, which incorrectly used `p.unit` ('in') instead of `buildingUnit` ('ft').
  * This converted a 10 ft pillar as a 10 inch pillar (1/12th of actual volume), yielding 4.2 CFT for 9 pillars instead of 50.625 CFT.
- Fix Implemented:
  * `pH_M` now converts height in `p.heightUnit || buildingUnit || 'ft'`.
  * Individual pillar volume (9" × 9" × 10 ft) = (0.75 ft × 0.75 ft × 10 ft) = 5.625 CFT = 0.15928 m³.
  * Total for 9 pillars = 9 × 5.625 CFT = 50.625 CFT = 1.43354 m³.
  * Cement for 9 pillars = 1.43354 × 1.54 × (1 / 5.5) / 0.0347 ≈ 11.57 Bags (~12 Bags, replacing the erroneous 1 Bag).
  * M-Sand for 9 pillars ≈ 21.3 CFT; Jalli for 9 pillars ≈ 42.5 CFT.
- Full RCC Project Recalculation:
  * Sum of all 9 individual pillar volumes correctly processed and aggregated into Total RCC Concrete, Cement, Sand, Jalli, Steel, and Total RCC Cost.
